Hot Corners

Rest the pointer in a corner of the page and the action you picked for it runs.

Hot Corners is ready

Try it now: move the pointer to the top-left corner of this page to see all your tabs.

  • The top-left corner opens the tab overview and the bottom-right corner jumps back to your last tab. Change them below.
  • Turn everything off or on with . The toolbar icon turns grey while it's off.
  • Right-click the toolbar icon to pause for 15 minutes or an hour. It comes back on by itself.
  • Pin Hot Corners from Chrome's puzzle-piece menu to keep the icon in view.

Corners

Pick an action for each corner. Add a key to hold if you like: that corner then works only while the key is down, so a stray pointer never sets it off.

Page area Corners are the corners of the page below the tab bar.
In full screen (F11) they match the corners of your screen.

Behavior

How long the pointer rests in a corner before the action runs.

How close to the edge counts as the corner. Smaller means fewer accidental triggers.

A short flash in the corner shows that the action ran.

Corners rest while a page is in full screen, such as a video or a game.

Keyboard shortcuts

Turn Hot Corners on or off

Stops everything with one key; press again to bring it back.

Open or close the tab overview

Shows every tab without going to a corner.

Change or remove these on Chrome's extension shortcuts page.

Excluded sites

Corners never fire on these sites. You can also add the current site from the toolbar icon.
