Hot Corners Privacy Policy

Chrome extension · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Nothing collectedNo personal data, browsing history or page content is collected.
No serversThe extension sends no data anywhere and contains no analytics or ads.
Stays on your deviceSettings live in Chrome; tab previews stay in memory for the session.

What the extension handles

Hot Corners runs the browser actions you choose when the pointer rests in a corner of a page. To do that, it handles the following information inside your own browser only:

  • Your settings: the action for each corner, the delay, the corner size and the sites you excluded. They are stored with chrome.storage.sync; if Chrome Sync is on, Chrome syncs them between your own devices through your Google account. That sync is a Chrome feature; the extension has no other access to this data. The pause state stays on the current device in chrome.storage.local.
  • Pointer position: checked inside the page to tell whether the pointer is in a corner. It is never stored.
  • Tab titles and addresses: read to show the tab overview and to switch tabs. They are never stored.
  • Tab previews: for the thumbnails in the tab overview, the visible tab is captured, shrunk and kept in chrome.storage.session. That storage lives in memory only and is cleared when the tab or the browser closes.

Sharing

None of this information is sent to the developer or to any third party, and none of it is sold or transferred. The extension loads no remote code and makes no network requests.

Your control

You can turn the extension off, exclude specific sites or uninstall it at any time. Uninstalling removes its settings.
